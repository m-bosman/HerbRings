# Changelog

## 0.8.0
- Herbs you pick at a spot Questie doesn't have are saved and get a ring too. Turn off in the settings or with `/hrings finds off`.
- `/hrings forget` removes your finds near you, `/hrings forget all` removes them all.

## 0.7.2
- Default range is now 400 yards.

## 0.7.1
- First public version.
- Rings on herb spawn points from Questie, coloured by skill-up chance.
- Ring tooltips and world tooltip colouring.
- Settings panel: colours, hiding per colour or per herb, custom colour per herb.
